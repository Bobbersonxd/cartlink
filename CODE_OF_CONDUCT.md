# Code of conduct (Cartlink)

Cartlink is a small volunteer project: an unofficial fork of RetroArch.
Everyone who takes part in its issues, pull requests and discussions is
expected to:

- be respectful and patient, also when you disagree;
- keep to the topic, and give others the benefit of the doubt;
- not harass, insult, threaten or single out anyone, and not post other
  people's private information;
- not ask for, share or link to copyrighted games (ROMs) or BIOS files.

The maintainers may edit or remove posts, close threads, and block
people who don't follow this, for as long as they see fit.

## Reporting

To report a problem with someone's behaviour, open an issue at
https://github.com/Bobbersonxd/cartlink/issues, or, if it should not be
public, use the private report form described in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) (the repository's **Security** tab →
**Report a vulnerability**) and say that it is a conduct report. Reports
are read only by the maintainers and kept confidential.

This code covers Cartlink's own spaces only. RetroArch and libretro have
their own; Cartlink is not part of them, so please don't send Cartlink
reports there.
