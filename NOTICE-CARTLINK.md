## Bundled mbedTLS (`deps/mbedtls`)

RetroArch's mbedTLS 2.6.0 copy was replaced with the unmodified Mbed TLS
2.28.10 release (Apache-2.0, `deps/mbedtls/LICENSE`) except for
`deps/mbedtls/mbedtls/config.h` (Cartlink build settings, marked
"Modified by Cartlink contributors, 2026") and the Cartlink file
`deps/mbedtls/sources.mk`. Source, SHA-256 and the exact config
changes: `deps/mbedtls/CARTLINK-VERSION.txt`. 160 files under
`deps/mbedtls/` differ from the upstream RetroArch commit.
